## Speaker Bio

Zach Watkins is a software engineer at Texas Transportation Institute with 19 years of experience providing websites and web applications for the public and private sector. He has a BFA and has done everything from website designs and front-end coding to building APIs with job queues and end-to-end test automation. He has been a public speaker on technical subjects since 2015 and in his current role he works with civil engineers and researchers to save time, lives, and resources by developing custom technology solutions for transportation research grants.
